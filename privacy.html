Privacy policy

Your recordings and transcripts never leave your device. InkWhisper transcribes inside your browser and does not upload audio, video or text anywhere.

Who is responsible

[your name], [address], [your email].

The transcriber

  • Recordings and transcripts are processed only in your browser. When you close the page they are gone, unless you saved them.
  • License key. When you enter or re-check your key, the page sends the key and a label such as “Chrome on Windows” to Polar, so the key can be limited to your allowed number of browsers. The key is saved in your browser so you don’t have to enter it again.
  • Speech models are downloaded once from Hugging Face and the transcription engine from jsDelivr, then kept in your browser. Like any download, this shows your IP address to those services.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Buying

Payment happens in Polar’s checkout. Polar, as the Merchant of Record, collects your name, email, billing country and payment details to process the order, prevent fraud and handle tax. We receive your name, email and order details from Polar so we can deliver the license and answer support requests. We never see your full card number. See Polar’s privacy policy.

This website

The website has no analytics and sets no tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs and jsDelivr, which see your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you and keep the conversation as long as needed for support and legal records.

How long we keep data

Order records are kept as long as accounting law requires. You can ask us to delete anything else at any time.

Your rights
