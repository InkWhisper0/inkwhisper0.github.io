        Which languages does it understand?
        Whisper supports 99 languages, including English, Spanish, German and French. Accuracy depends on the language and the recording quality. We will publish test results before launch.
        All 99: .
        
        
        Do I need a powerful computer?
        No. A recent computer is enough. In Chrome and Edge InkWhisper uses your graphics card, which is much faster. Phones work too, but long recordings are slow.
        
        
        How long can a recording be?
        Free: up to 10 minutes per recording. With a license there is no limit. Very long recordings simply take longer.
        
        
        Can it tell who is speaking?
        The first version does not separate speakers. The transcript contains the text and timestamps.
        
        
        How many devices can I use it on?
        The license is for one person in . To move it, open the license window, remove it from the old browser and enter the key in the new one.
        
        
        Can I try it before I buy?
        Yes. Recordings up to 10 minutes are free, with no account. Try it with your own files first, because all sales are final.
        
        
        Is this an OpenAI product?
        No. InkWhisper is an independent product. It uses Whisper, an open speech recognition model that OpenAI released under the MIT license.
        
        
        Does it work on a Mac or a phone?
        Yes. It runs in a modern browser on Windows, Mac and Linux. Phones work too, but slowly.
        
        
        Is my recording really not uploaded?
        Yes. Your browser downloads the speech model once, then does all the work on your device. The file you drop in never leaves it. Only the license check talks to a server, and it sends just your key.
